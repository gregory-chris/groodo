# Future feature ideas

## Projects page

1. **Backlog lanes: Ideas → Next Up → In Progress → Done**  
   - Simple Kanban-style lanes inside each project focused on "what to build next".  
   - Drag tasks between lanes to quickly groom the backlog and surface the true "Next Up" list.
2. **Lightweight priority & scoring**  
   - Add a tiny priority indicator (e.g. P1 / P2 / P3 or "Must / Nice") plus an optional score (impact vs. effort).  
   - Sort and filter the project task list by priority/score to see the best next bets for the project.
3. **Idea inbox & quick capture**  
   - A dedicated "Idea inbox" per project where you can quickly jot down messy ideas without over-structuring them.  
   - Later, you can convert ideas into proper tasks and drop them into the right place in the hierarchy.
4. **"Build next" shortlist**  
   - A small, focused shortlist (e.g. up to 5 tasks) marked as "Build next" for each project.  
   - Visually emphasized in the tasks panel so you always see the few things you’ve committed to tackle next.
5. **Project–week links**  
   - Ability to flag a project task as "scheduled this week" and see which tasks from that project are already on the weekly planner.  
   - Quick action to send a project task directly into a specific day on the weekly planner.
6. **Per-project notes & decisions**  
   - A simple notes area in the details panel for capturing decisions, trade-offs, and mini-roadmaps in free text.  
   - Option to pin key notes at the top so they stay visible while you groom tasks.
7. **Archiving & seasonal projects**  
   - Archive projects you’re not actively working on, without losing their tasks/ideas.  
   - Filter to show only "Active" projects to keep the interface focused on your current pet projects.

## Weekly tasks page

1. **Yesterday–Today–Tomorrow strip**  
   - A compact view that highlights just yesterday, today, and tomorrow, with easy drag-and-drop between them.  
   - Makes it frictionless to see what you just finished, what’s in front of you, and what’s immediately next.
2. **Daily focus spotlight**  
   - Mark 1–3 tasks as "Today’s focus" and visually spotlight them at the top of today’s column.  
   - Subtle cues (color, icon) to keep your attention anchored on the most important work for your day job.
3. **Carry-over helper**  
   - One-click actions to move unfinished tasks from today to tomorrow (or later in the week) in bulk or selectively.  
   - Optional hint if there are still open tasks late in the workday, making it easy to clean up and reschedule.
4. **Lightweight daily log**  
   - A simple "What I actually did" log that automatically lists completed tasks for yesterday and today.  
   - Great for end-of-day reflection and for reconstructing status updates at work.
5. **Workday boundaries & quiet hours (visual only)**  
   - Soft visual boundary for your typical work hours, without heavy time-blocking or scheduling overhead.  
   - Helps distinguish between core focus hours and overflow work, while keeping the UI lightweight.
6. **Quick add from projects to a day**  
   - From the weekly view, quickly search or browse project tasks and drop them into a specific day.  
   - Encourages a natural flow: decide what to pull from pet projects into your limited work focus slots.
7. **Weekly snapshot sidebar**  
   - A small summary showing how many tasks you completed yesterday, today, and so far this week.  
   - Keeps you aware of progress without turning the planner into a heavy analytics dashboard.
